Finds Studio

Turn a song you love into a vertical music video.

Finds Studio is a music-video tool by Wellspring Studio LLC for music discovery creators. Paste a public SoundCloud song link, pick a clip, and it builds a 1080 × 1920 video with the cover art, artist credit, an audio-driven visualizer, synchronized lyric captions, and a ready-to-post description.

How it works

  1. Import a SoundCloud song and choose a 3–60 second clip.
  2. Generate lyric captions with built-in speech recognition, then review and edit them.
  3. Preview and export the MP4. An attribution description is generated automatically.
  4. Optionally connect TikTok and click Send to TikTok drafts. The video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox, where you add the caption and decide whether to post it.

Private by design

The studio is a password-protected tool. Audio, transcription, and rendering run on our own server, and videos are only sent to TikTok when you choose to.

Finds Studio is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SoundCloud or TikTok.
