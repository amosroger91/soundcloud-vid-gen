Finds Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Finds Studio ("the app") is open-source software that runs entirely on your own computer. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Information the app processes

  • Songs and artwork you import. The audio, cover art, title, and artist of the SoundCloud links you paste are downloaded and stored on your computer.
  • Videos and captions you create. Rendered videos, lyric transcripts, and descriptions are stored on your computer. Speech recognition runs on your device.
  • TikTok account data. If you connect TikTok, the app receives your TikTok open ID and display name (through the user.info.basic scope) plus access and refresh tokens. These are stored only in a file on your computer and are used only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you choose.

How information is shared

Data is sent to a third party only when you take an action that needs it:

  • TikTok receives your sign-in request when you connect your account, and the video you select when you click "Send to TikTok drafts" (through the video.upload scope). TikTok's handling of that data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • SoundCloud receives requests for the song links you import.
  • On first use, the app downloads an open speech-recognition model from Hugging Face. No audio is uploaded.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Click Disconnect in the app to revoke the TikTok tokens and delete them from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in your TikTok account settings.
  • Delete the app's data folder to remove all imported songs, videos, and stored tokens.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. The TikTok features are only available to people who meet TikTok's age requirements.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
