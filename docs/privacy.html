Finds Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Finds Studio ("the app") is operated by Wellspring Studio LLC ("we") at finds.wellspringstudiollc.com. It is a private, password-protected tool. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Information the app processes

  • Songs and artwork you import. The audio, cover art, title, and artist of the SoundCloud links you paste are downloaded and stored on our server.
  • Videos and captions you create. Rendered videos, lyric transcripts, and descriptions are stored on our server. Speech recognition runs on our server; audio is not sent to any speech or AI service.
  • TikTok account data. If you connect TikTok, the app receives your TikTok open ID and display name (through the user.info.basic scope) plus access and refresh tokens. These are stored only on our server and are used only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you choose.

How information is shared

Data is sent to a third party only when you take an action that needs it:

  • TikTok receives your sign-in request when you connect your account, and the video you select when you click "Send to TikTok drafts" (through the video.upload scope). TikTok's handling of that data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • SoundCloud receives requests for the song links you import.
  • On first use, the app downloads an open speech-recognition model from Hugging Face. No audio is uploaded.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Click Disconnect in the app to revoke the TikTok tokens and delete them from our server. You can also remove the app's access in your TikTok account settings.
  • Email contact@wellspringstudiollc.com to have your imported songs, videos, and stored tokens deleted.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. The TikTok features are only available to people who meet TikTok's age requirements.

Changes

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
